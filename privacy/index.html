Privacy policy

For Sankalpa and Vāṇī, made by Tattva Apps. Last updated November 15, 2026.

We made these apps for devotees, as a service. We do not show ads, we do not sell or share your information, and we collect only what the apps need to work.

Sankalpa

When you join Sankalpa we keep:

  • Your name and your Bhakti Vriksha group, so your group can see you on its board.
  • Your email address and a PIN, so you can sign in on any phone. The PIN is stored scrambled (hashed), never as you typed it.
  • Your sankalpas and the days you ticked them, sick days, and whether you marked yourself as a child, to work out your score and streak.

Who can see it. Other members of your group see only your name, score, streak and a “Kid” tag if you chose one. Your Bhakti Vriksha leader and the temple organizer can also see your sankalpas and progress, to encourage you. Nobody else can.

Where it is kept. In a private Google Sheet run by Tattva Apps (Google stores it securely). Your phone also keeps a copy so the app works quickly.

Reminders. In the iPhone app, the daily reminder is set up on your phone itself. No reminder information is sent to us.

Emails. We may email you a gentle reminder about your sankalpas or important news about the app. Reply to any email to ask us to stop.

Vāṇī

Vāṇī does not ask for your name, email or any account. To know how many people listen, it keeps an anonymous random code for each phone, which station was played and for how many minutes. It does not store your name, email or IP address.

Children

Children are welcome in Sankalpa with a parent's or guardian's permission. A parent or guardian should set up the account for a child under 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from children beyond what is listed above, and a parent can ask us to delete a child's account at any time.

Deleting your information

In Sankalpa, open My sankalpas → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your name, sign-in, sankalpas and history. You can also email us and we will delete it for you within 7 days.
