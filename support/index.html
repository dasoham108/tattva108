Support

Help with Sankalpa and Vāṇī.

Email us at sankalpaadmin.108@gmail.com and we will reply within a few days.

Sankalpa

  • Forgot your PIN? Ask your Bhakti Vriksha leader. They can set a new PIN for you from their leader page.
  • Using a new phone? Open Sankalpa, choose Sign in, and use your email and PIN. Everything comes back.
  • Were you unwell? On the Today page, scroll to the bottom and tap Unwell today? Mark it as a sick day. Sick days do not count against your score or streak.
  • Daily reminder (iPhone app): open My sankalpas and switch on Daily reminder. If no reminder comes, allow notifications for Sankalpa in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Delete your account: My sankalpas → Delete my account.

Vāṇī

  • Sound stops or doesn't move to the next track? Close Vāṇī completely and open it again to get the latest version.

Our privacy policy explains what information the apps keep.
